Code of Conduct

The Blood Pact

Those who break these rules forfeit their place in the BloodBound world.

✦ The Laws of the Void
  • I. Staff never abuses their rank for personal gain on the server.
  • II. Decisions are made with reasoning and never out of emotion. Players deserve fair treatment.
  • III. Internal communication stays internal. What is discussed in the staff channel remains there.
  • IV. Every staff action (ban, kick, mute) is logged with a clear reason.
  • V. When in doubt, consult a higher rank — never act alone on major decisions.
  • VI. Activity requirement: at least 5 hours online per week. Inactivity without notice will lead to demotion.
  • VII. Developer, Co-Owner, and Owner positions are granted exclusively by the current leadership. Requests through public channels will be ignored.
